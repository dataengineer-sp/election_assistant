Quick reference

🌍

Voting systems worldwide

  • First-past-the-post UK, India, US
  • Proportional representation Germany, NL
  • Ranked-choice voting Australia
  • Two-round system France
  • Electoral college US only
📅

Typical election timeline

  • Election announced 6–12 mo. prior
  • Registration closes 30–90 days
  • Campaigning ends 48 hrs before
  • Polls open Election day
  • Results certified 2–6 weeks after
✅

Eligibility checklist

  • Citizen of the country Required
  • Minimum age (18 most) Required
  • Registered voter Required
  • Valid ID (varies by country) Check local
  • Not legally disqualified Required
🔒

Your voting rights

  • Secret ballot guaranteed Protected
  • Free from intimidation Protected
  • Assistance if needed Accessibility
  • Provisional ballot option Most countries
  • Right to observe counting Via observers
